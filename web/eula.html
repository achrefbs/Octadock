2. Devices and activation

Your license may be active on up to 3 devices at a time. Activation associates a device with your license using a one-way device hash (described in the privacy policy). If you replace or reinstall a machine, its slot can be freed so you are not permanently locked out. Support can help you move a slot if you get stuck.

3. Updates

Your license includes 12 months of updates from your purchase date, and this window includes the 1.0 release. After the 12 months end, the version you have keeps working; your license does not stop. You simply stop receiving new updates unless you choose to renew.

Renewal is optional: for $19 per year you can extend your updates by another 12 months. You are never required to renew to keep using the software you already have.

Some optional features connect to third-party services: confirmed AI Actions use the Codex or Claude CLI you select, cloud speech-to-text uses OpenAI, and cloud voices use ElevenLabs. These are off by default.

When you enable them, you use your own account and API keys with those providers, and they bill you directly for your usage under their own terms. We are not a party to your relationship with those providers, we do not set their prices, and we are not responsible for their charges, availability, or output. You are responsible for keeping your keys secure and for your usage of those services.
