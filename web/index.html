One fast path from screen to action

Capture and dictation are the daily loop. Context and AI Actions turn those local artifacts into something you can safely use next.

Capture & shelf

Area, window, full-screen, timer, and manual scrolling capture. Each shot docks as a card to copy, save, annotate, pin, or drag straight into another app.

OCR

Pull text out of any region or image with the local Windows OCR engine, copied to your clipboard as compact, line, or layout output.

Dictation one-time model download

Speech-to-text at the cursor. The engine runs on your PC after a one-time model download; a cloud speech option is opt-in and billed by your provider.

Read aloud

Local-first, stated precisely

Your captures never leave your PC

Screenshots, recordings, OCR text, history, and clipboard entries are stored locally. Octadock does not upload them.

Dictation needs a one-time model download

The first time you dictate, Octadock downloads a speech model from Hugging Face. It runs on your PC after that. You are shown the size and can cancel.

AI Actions use the cloud service behind your selected CLI

Octadock shows the exact outbound text, redacts common secrets by default, names the Codex or Claude destination, and asks again before sending. Your provider handles and bills the request.

Optional OpenAI & ElevenLabs are opt-in

Cloud speech-to-text (OpenAI) and cloud voices (ElevenLabs) are off by default. When you enable them with your own key, that provider handles the request and bills you.
