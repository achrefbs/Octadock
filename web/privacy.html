The short version

Octadock is a local-first desktop app. Your screenshots, recordings, OCR text, clipboard history, and app history are stored on your PC and are never uploaded by Octadock. The only personal data we hold on a server is what the license service needs to sell you a license and let you activate it. That data, and every network call the app can make, is enumerated below.

What the desktop app stores (all local)

The following live only on your computer, in your local Octadock data. Octadock does not transmit them:

  • Screenshots and screen recordings you capture.
  • Annotations and .octadock project files you create.
  • Capture history and the action log (a local SQLite database).
  • Clipboard history entries.
  • OCR text extracted from images or regions.
  • Floating pin positions and app settings.

Deleting these on your machine deletes them everywhere, because there is no copy on our side.

What the license service stores

When you buy a license and when you activate it, the Octadock license service records the fields below. This is the complete list of personal data we hold on a server:

FieldWhy we store it
License keyThe credential that unlocks the app and identifies your purchase.
Purchase emailTo deliver your key and let you look up or recover your license.
Stripe customer IDLinks your license to your payment record for support and refunds.
Stripe Checkout session IDLets the confirmation page show your key right after payment.
Stripe payment-intent IDTies a specific payment to a specific license for reconciliation and refunds.
Device hashes / machine hashA one-way hash derived from a machine identifier, used to count and manage your 3 device slots. It is not your hardware serial and cannot be reversed into it.
Activation timestampsWhen each device activated, so device-limit and update-window logic works.
Audit logAn append-only record of license changes (issue, activate, deactivate, revoke, reinstate) for support and dispute handling.

We do not store your captures, your OCR text, your clipboard, your dictation audio, or your app history on the license service. Those never reach it.

Processors we use

Two third parties process data on our behalf:

Stripe
Payment processing and the customer record behind your license. Stripe receives your payment details and the email you check out with. We never see or store your full card number.
FastMail
Our email provider, on the mail.octadock.com subdomain. It handles delivery of your license key and support correspondence, so it processes your email address and message contents.

When you turn on the opt-in cloud features, your chosen provider (OpenAI or ElevenLabs) processes the data you send them under their own terms and bills you directly. They are your providers, not ours.

Retention

  • License records (key, email, Stripe IDs, device hashes, activation timestamps, audit log) are kept for the life of the license so activation, updates, support, and refunds keep working, and afterward as needed to meet tax and accounting obligations.
  • Support email is kept as long as needed to resolve your request and for a reasonable period afterward.
  • Local app data has no server retention because it never leaves your PC; you control it with the app's own history retention settings and by deleting files.

Your rights & contact

You can ask us to access, correct, or erase the personal data the license service holds about you. Because your identity is your license key plus purchase email, that is what we use to find your record. Erasing your license record will deactivate the license.

To make a request, or to ask anything about this policy, email support@octadock.com.
