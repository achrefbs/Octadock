PLAINLY, AND IN ONE PLACE

Your screen stays yours.

The desktop app works locally. Optional website emails are separate from the app and are never needed to download or use it.

On your PC

Captures, annotations, recordings, history, settings and logs live under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Octadock. Context bundles go to a folder you choose. Clipboard history is off by default.

The app has no account, telemetry, automatic updater or cloud processing. Other software may sync your chosen folders; Octadock does not upload them.

Voice and text

OCR uses the Windows engine. Read-aloud uses installed Windows voices. Dictation uses a Parakeet or Whisper model that you import from disk, then processes speech on your PC.

This website

Railway hosts this website and receives ordinary web requests, including IP addresses and browser information. There are no analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts and page assets are served from this website.

Optional promotional emails

If you actively subscribe, your email address and consent record are used for Octadock news, tips and promotional offers from the creator. Subscribing is optional and does not affect your download or app features.

We do not receive your captures or other app data when you subscribe. We do not sell the email list. You can withdraw your consent using the unsubscribe link in an email, or ask for access, correction or deletion at support@octadock.com.

Your email and dated consent record are stored privately on the Octadock website’s Railway service. No campaign delivery provider is connected yet, and the signup form does not send a campaign. Active signup records expire after 24 months. Unsubscribing removes the email address and keeps only a one-way fingerprint to prevent accidental re-addition. Hosting backups may retain older copies for their backup retention period.

Contact

Support messages are received through Fastmail and used to respond to your request. Support email is separate from the optional promotional mailing list.

Octadock is an independent project by Achref Boularess. For privacy questions, contact support@octadock.com.
